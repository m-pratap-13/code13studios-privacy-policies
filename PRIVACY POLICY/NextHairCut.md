# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 25, 2026

Welcome to **Next Hair Cut – Men's Salon**.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

---

# 1. Information We Collect

When you use our application, we may collect the following information:

- Full Name
- Email Address
- Phone Number
- Profile Photo (Optional)
- Appointment History
- Booking Preferences
- Device information required for app functionality

---

# 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Create and manage your account.
- Process salon appointment bookings.
- Display your profile within the application.
- Provide customer support.
- Improve application performance and user experience.
- Send booking confirmations and important service notifications.

---

# 3. Technologies We Use

To securely operate our application, we use the following trusted services:

### Firebase Authentication
Used for secure user authentication and account management.

### Firebase Firestore
Used to store user accounts, bookings, application settings, and other application data.

### Supabase Storage
Used to securely store profile photos and other uploaded images.

---

# 4. Data Sharing

We respect your privacy.

**We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.**

Your information is only processed by trusted infrastructure providers, including Firebase and Supabase, solely for the purpose of operating, maintaining, and securing the application.

---

# 5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information.

Our application relies on the security infrastructure provided by Firebase and Supabase, together with appropriate safeguards, to help prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction of your personal information.

---

# 6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- View your personal information.
- Update or correct your information.
- Delete your account.
- Request permanent deletion of your personal data from our servers, subject to applicable legal requirements.

---

# 7. Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to:

- Provide our services.
- Maintain your account.
- Comply with applicable legal obligations.

Once your account is deleted and no legal or operational retention is required, your personal information will be permanently removed from our systems.
