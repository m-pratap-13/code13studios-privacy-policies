Privacy Policy

My Tuition Fees

Last Updated: June 2026

Welcome to My Tuition Fees. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, stored, and used within the application.

Information We Collect

The application may store information entered by the user, including:

  • Full Name
  • Contact Number
  • Student Information
  • Fee Records
  • Other tuition-related information entered by the user

Local Data Storage

All information entered into My Tuition Fees is stored locally on the user's device.

The application does not currently upload, transmit, or store user data on external servers, cloud services, or databases.

The developer does not have access to any data stored within the application on your device.

How Information Is Used

The information is used solely for:

  • Managing tuition fee records
  • Maintaining student information
  • Providing core app functionality
  • Improving the user experience

Data Sharing

My Tuition Fees does not sell, rent, share, or distribute personal information to third parties.

Advertising

The application currently does not display advertisements.

In the future, advertisements may be introduced through third-party advertising providers. If this occurs, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Children's Privacy

This application is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise app usage by minors.

Data Security

Since all information is stored locally on the user's device, users are responsible for maintaining the security of their devices and backups.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
